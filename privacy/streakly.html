Privacy Policy — Streakly: Habit Tracker

Effective date: September 21, 2026

Streakly: Habit Tracker ("we", "our", or "us") helps you build daily habits with streak tracking, gentle reminders, and satisfying check-ins. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Information you enter. Habit names, colors, goal notes, and check-in dates are stored on your device only, using your device's local storage. We do not collect, transmit, or store your entries on any server.

We do not require an account and do not collect your name, email address, location, or other personal identifiers.

How We Use Information

Your entries are used only to display your habits, streaks, and statistics inside the app, on your device. We do not sell, share, or transfer your information to any third party.

Third-Party Services

The app may display banner ads served by Google AdMob. In the current version, only Google's official sample/test ad units are used — no real ads are served and no advertising identifiers are collected for real ad targeting. If real AdMob advertising is enabled in a future version, Google may collect device information as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

The app does not use any third-party analytics SDKs.

Notifications

If you enable the optional daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No reminder data leaves your device.

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Choices

Because we do not collect or store your personal information on our servers, there is no account data for us to show, correct, or delete. You can erase all app data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
